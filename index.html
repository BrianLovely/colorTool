WeCo HSL Color Palette Generator

The generator allow you to build a set of color by setting the hue, saturation, and lightness of each color. Hue is the color itself, saturation is how vivid and pure the color is, and lightness is how dark or light the color is.

The hues are represented by a circle, so the values will be a degree from 0 to 360. For example, red is 0 (or 360,) brown is around 25 to 50, yellow is 60, green is 120, and blue is 240.

Saturation and lightness are expressed in percentages. A saturation of 100 will be a bright clear color, and the closer it is set to 0 the color will be more dull. Similarly a lightness of 50 will be the most pure color, higher values mix the color with white, and lower mix the color with black. 100% lightness is white, and 0% lightness is black.
